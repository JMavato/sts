WHO WE ARE

Our Journey & Commitment

Welcome to Simple Tech Services

Simple Tech Services is a 100% Papua New Guinea-owned technology company established in 2019 by Mr. Pau Ukuma and officially registered with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA) in 2020. Based in Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea.

We provide professional IT support, computer repairs, networking solutions, website development, printing services, and digital business solutions to individuals, small businesses, and organisations throughout Papua New Guinea.

Overcoming Challenges & Relocation

Like many businesses, we were affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. Our operations were placed on hold, and our relocation from Popondetta to Port Moresby took longer than expected.

Following our transition to Port Moresby, Simple Tech Services expanded its service portfolio and strengthened its technical capabilities to better serve clients throughout Papua New Guinea.

Whether you need a quick repair, professional printing, or a new website for your business, we are ready to help. Thank you for trusting a local PNG business.
